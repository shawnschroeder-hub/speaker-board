Before you buy: three things about hotel rooms

1. Ask the hotel first

Most hotel conference rooms already have ceiling speakers with a laptop hookup (HDMI or 3.5mm) at the podium or wall plate. Ask the event coordinator for "house sound with a laptop input." It is often free or a small AV fee, and it beats anything on this list for even coverage. Bring your own speaker as the backup.

2. Use a cable for video, Bluetooth for music

Bluetooth adds roughly a tenth of a second of delay. Fine for music and slides, but on a video clip the lips get ahead of the sound. Every pick above has a wired input: 3.5mm on the Bose and PartyBox, USB-C on the Charge 6. Pack a 3.5mm cable and a USB-C to 3.5mm adapter for the laptop ($10–20 total).

3. Skip anything that needs Wi-Fi

Sonos speakers link over Wi-Fi, and hotel Wi-Fi with a login page will not let them join. Unless you bring your own travel router, choose a Bluetooth or wired option for a hotel. That is why Sonos sits lower on this board despite excellent sound.

Setup on meeting day

One-box setup (Bose S1 Pro+)

  1. Charge it overnight; it also runs on wall power.
  2. Set it at the front of the room on a table or stand, tilted toward the far end. Avoid corners.
  3. Run the 3.5mm cable from the laptop headphone jack (or USB-C adapter) into the aux input. Or hold the Bluetooth button and pair from the laptop.
  4. Set the speaker to about half volume, then set the laptop to full. Adjust from the speaker's knob during the meeting.
  5. Test with a 20-second video clip from the back of the room 30 minutes before start.

3–4 speaker setup (JBL Charge 6 or PartyBox)

  1. Charge all units; label them 1–4 with tape.
  2. Place one near each end of the table and one on each long side, at ear height, pointed inward.
  3. Connect speaker 1 to the laptop (USB-C cable or Bluetooth).
  4. Press the Auracast button on speaker 1, then on each other speaker. They join within a few seconds. The JBL Portable app shows the group and lets you balance volumes.
  5. Keep total volume low. Four speakers at 30% sound better and clearer than one at 90%.
Buy the same model for all units. JBL says any Auracast-enabled JBL speaker links with any other, but older PartyBoost models (Charge 5, Flip 6, Xtreme 3) will not join.
